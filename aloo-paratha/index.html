Aloo Paratha Reimagined

Transforming the humble Indian staple into a global culinary experience. Discover four unique ways to elevate your leftover Aloo Parathas into something extraordinary.

01. Breakfast Quesadilla

Ingredients

2 pre-made Aloo Parathas, 3 large eggs, 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar, 1/4 cup diced bell peppers, 2 tbsp red onions, 1 tbsp butter, salt/pepper, side of salsa.

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with veggies and seasonings.
  2. Scramble eggs in butter; remove from heat.
  3. Layer eggs and cheese between two parathas.
  4. Press-grill until golden-brown and crispy.

02. Street-Style Chaat

Ingredients

2 Aloo Parathas, 1 cup Greek yogurt, 1/4 cup tamarind chutney, 1/4 cup mint-cilantro chutney, 1/2 cup sev, onions, pomegranate, chaat masala, coriander.

Method

  1. Crisp the parathas and cut into bite-sized squares.
  2. Arrange in a bowl and drizzle whisked yogurt.
  3. Add chutneys and sprinkle with onions, spices, and pomegranate.
  4. Finish with sev and fresh coriander garnish.

03. Paneer Kathi Roll

Ingredients

2 Aloo Parathas, 200g Paneer strips, onion, cabbage, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, Kashmiri chili, lemon juice, mint chutney.

Method

  1. Sauté paneer with spices and ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Warm parathas and spread with mint chutney.
  3. Add paneer, raw onions, and cabbage.
  4. Roll tightly and slice.

04. Pizza Margherita

Ingredients

1 Aloo Paratha, 3 tbsp pizza sauce, 1/2 cup fresh mozzarella, fresh basil leaves, olive oil, oregano, red chili flakes.

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Spread sauce and top with mozzarella.
  3. Bake until bubbly (8-10 mins).
  4. Finish with basil, olive oil, and seasoning.
