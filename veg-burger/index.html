Beyond the Patty

Elevating the humble veggie patty into gourmet culinary experiences. From classic comfort to Mediterranean vibrancy, discover the versatility of plant-forward cooking.

1. The Toasted Brioche Garden Burger

Ingredients:

  • 1 Veggie patty, 1 Brioche bun
  • 1 slice Sharp cheddar, 2 leaves Butter lettuce
  • 2 slices Large tomato, 3-4 thin Red onion slices
  • 3 Dill pickle chips, 1 tbsp Salted butter
  • 1 tbsp Mayo, 1 tsp Mustard, 1 tsp Ketchup

Instructions:

  1. Toast bun halves in melted butter until golden.
  2. Pan-sear patty 4-5 mins per side; melt cheese on top.
  3. Spread condiments; layer greens, patty, and pickles.

2. Mediterranean Harvest Grain Bowl

Ingredients:

  • 1 Veggie patty, 1 cup Quinoa/Brown rice
  • 2 cups Spinach, ½ cup Cherry tomatoes
  • ¼ cup Cucumber, 2 tbsp Feta, 5-6 Olives
  • 1 tbsp Hummus, 1 tbsp Olive oil, ½ Lemon
  • Dried oregano, Salt, Pepper

Instructions:

  1. Crumble and sauté patty chunks until crispy.
  2. Whisk dressing with lemon, oil, and herbs.
  3. Arrange grains, greens, and veggies; top with patty, feta, and hummus.

3. Smoky Veggie Street Tacos

Ingredients:

  • 1 Veggie patty, 3 Tortillas
  • 1 cup Shredded cabbage, ½ Avocado
  • Fresh cilantro, Lime wedges, Sour cream
  • Chili powder, Cumin, Oil

Instructions:

  1. Dice patty, sauté with chili and cumin.
  2. Whisk sour cream with lime and salt.
  3. Assemble tortillas with spiced patty, cabbage, avocado, and crema.

4. Savory Veggie Patty & Potato Hash

Ingredients:

  • 1 Veggie patty, 1 Russet potato
  • ½ Bell pepper, ¼ Onion, 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 Egg, Smoked paprika, Olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, Salt, Pepper

Instructions:

  1. Microwave potatoes, then brown in skillet.
  2. Add onion, peppers, and crumbled patty.
  3. Create well, add egg, cover until set. Garnish and serve.
