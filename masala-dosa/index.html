Classic Golden Masala Dosa

Ingredients

Batter: 3 cups fermented rice/lentil batter, salt, water. Potato Masala: 4 boiled potatoes, 2 onions, 3 green chillies, mustard seeds, turmeric, curry leaves, oil/ghee.

Method

  1. Sauté spices and aromatics; add potatoes.
  2. Spread thin batter on hot tawa.
  3. Fry with ghee until crispy; fill and fold.

Masala Dosa Fusion Pizza

Ingredients

Base: 1.5 cups thick fermented batter. Toppings: 1 cup potato masala, onions, 1 cup mozzarella, tomato chutney, mustard seeds, curry leaves.

Method

  1. Cook base thick; spread chutney.
  2. Layer masala, onions, and cheese.
  3. Cover and steam until cheese melts; temper with mustard/curry leaves.

Spicy Potato Dosa Rolls

Ingredients

Dosa: 2 cups batter, ghee. Filling: 1.5 cups potato masala, 1 tbsp red chili garlic chutney, 0.5 cup carrots, onions, curry leaves.

Method

  1. Incorporate chutney into potato mix.
  2. Make extra crispy dosa; spread fillings.
  3. Roll tightly and slice diagonally.

Crispy Masala Dosa Waffles

Ingredients

Batter: 2 cups thick batter, onions, green chillies. Core: 1 cup mashed potato masala, mustard seeds, curry leaves, cooking spray.

Method

  1. Preheat waffle iron; mix aromatics in batter.
  2. Sandwich potato patty between batter layers.
  3. Cook 5-7 mins; garnish with infused oil.
