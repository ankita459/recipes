Upma Revival

Transform your humble leftover Upma into a gourmet masterpiece with these four creative culinary concepts.

Fridge Content Analysis

The Base Ingredients

Roasted Semolina (Sooji/Rava), Onions, Carrots, Green Peas, Green Chilies, Ginger, Mustard seeds, Cumin seeds, Urad dal, Chana dal, Curry leaves, Turmeric, Salt, Ghee, Fresh Cilantro, Lemon juice.

Chef's Philosophy

"Upma is a textured canvas. By integrating binding agents like potato or rice flour, we unlock textures ranging from crispy cutlets to hearty flatbreads."

1. Crispy Upma Cutlets

A savory, golden snack that transforms soft upma into crunchy patties.

Ingredients: 2 cups Upma, 2 boiled potatoes, 1/4 cup breadcrumbs, garam masala, chili powder, cilantro, cornstarch slurry.

Instructions: Combine base, shape into patties, coat in slurry/crumbs, and shallow fry until golden.

2. Cheesy Upma Sandwich

A quick and comforting breakfast twist using upma as a savory sandwich filling.

Ingredients: 1 cup Upma, 4 slices bread, cheddar cheese, green chutney, chaat masala, butter.

Instructions: Spread chutney and upma on bread, layer with cheese, toast until crisp and melted.

3. Golden Upma Masala Balls

Bite-sized, crunchy treats perfect for an evening snack.

Ingredients: 2 cups Upma, 1/2 cup rice flour, onions, ginger-garlic paste, chili flakes, curry leaves.

Instructions: Mix ingredients, roll into balls, and cook in an Appe pan or deep fry for maximum crunch.

4. Upma Stuffed Paratha

A hearty, filling flatbread where upma serves as a spicy, pre-cooked stuffing.

Ingredients: 1.5 cups Upma, 2 cups wheat flour, carom seeds, amchur powder, ghee.

Instructions: Prepare dough, stuff with seasoned upma, roll flat and sear on a hot tawa with ghee.
