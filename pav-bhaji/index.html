Pav Bhaji Arancini

Indian-Italian fusion: Spiced vegetable mash bound with rice, stuffed with molten cheese, and fried to golden perfection.

View Method & Ingredients ↓
  • 2 cups Pav Bhaji, 1 cup cooked rice
  • Mozzarella cubes, Panko breadcrumbs
  • Garlic-butter glaze

Form balls with cheese centers, coat in cornstarch & Panko. Deep fry until golden. Toss in garlic-butter and cilantro.

Deconstructed Shepherd’s Pie

A comforting casserole featuring a spicy vegetable base, buttery mash, and a crunchy Pav bread crust.

View Method & Ingredients ↓
  • Base: Mixed veg, tomatoes, masala
  • Topping: Mashed potato, butter, milk
  • Crust: Torn Pav chunks, oregano

Layer base and mash. Top with buttery Pav chunks. Bake at 400°F until bubbling.

Masala Pav Quesadillas

Crispy, cheesy tortilla-style wrap using flattened buns stuffed with the core Pav Bhaji flavors.

View Method & Ingredients ↓
  • 4 Pav buns (flattened)
  • Prepared Pav Bhaji, Red onions
  • Monterey Jack cheese, Mint chutney

Toast flattened buns on griddle. Spread chutney/bhaji, fold with cheese. Press until extremely crunchy.

Creamy Pav Bhaji Penne

A vibrant pasta dish where the traditional Bhaji is transformed into a rich, spiced cream sauce.

View Method & Ingredients ↓
  • 250g Penne, Heavy cream
  • Pureed Pav Bhaji, Parmesan
  • Kashmiri chili powder

Sauté garlic, add pureed bhaji, stir in cream. Toss with penne and Parmesan for a luxurious finish.
