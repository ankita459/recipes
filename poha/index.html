Poha Mastery

A curated collection of traditional and modern flattened rice delicacies.

Classic Kanda Batata Poha

A traditional savory breakfast dish featuring tempered flattened rice with onions and potatoes.

Ingredients

  • • 2 cups Thick Poha
  • • 1 medium Onion
  • • 1 medium Potato
  • • 1/4 cup Raw Peanuts
  • • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds

Instructions

Rinse poha and let rest. Heat oil, fry peanuts, temper mustard/cumin/curry leaves. Sauté potatoes and onions. Combine, steam for 2 mins, and garnish with lemon, cilantro, and sev.

Crispy Poha Veggie Cutlets

A crunchy, shallow-fried snack made by binding poha with mashed vegetables and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • • 1 cup Thick Poha
  • • 2 Potatoes, boiled
  • • 1/2 cup Mixed Veggies
  • • 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • • 2 tbsp Cornstarch

Instructions

Soften poha, mash with potatoes and vegetables. Add spices and binding agents. Shape into patties and shallow fry until deep golden brown. Serve hot.

Roasted Poha Chivda

A crunchy, shelf-stable snack made by roasting thin flattened rice with nuts and spices.

Ingredients

  • • 3 cups Thin Poha
  • • 1/2 cup Peanuts
  • • 1/4 cup Roasted Gram
  • • 15 Cashews
  • • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • • 1 tbsp Powdered Sugar

Instructions

Dry roast poha. Fry nuts, coconut, and spices in oil. Combine everything, toss gently to coat, and cool completely for maximum crunch.

Vella Poha

A quick-fix dessert or sweet snack utilizing flattened rice, unrefined cane sugar, and cardamom.

Ingredients

  • • 1.5 cups Poha
  • • 1/2 cup Jaggery
  • • 1/2 cup Fresh Coconut
  • • 1/4 tsp Cardamom
  • • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • • 1 tbsp Cashews & Raisins

Instructions

Soften poha. Mix with jaggery and coconut. Temper cashews and raisins in ghee and fold into the mixture. Serve as a quick sweet treat.
