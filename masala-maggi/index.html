Gourmet Maggi Lab

Elevating the humble instant noodle into a culinary experience. Four creative recipes to redefine your pantry staple.

1. Street-Style Vegetable Masala Maggi

A classic street food favorite, packed with fresh vegetables and warm spices.

View Ingredients & Method

Ingredients: 1 pkt Maggi, 1.5c water, onion, tomato, peas, green chili, turmeric, chili powder, oil, coriander.

Method: Sauté veggies and spices. Boil water, add noodles and tastemaker. Cook until thick, garnish with coriander.

2. Crispy Maggi Pakora Fritters

Crunchy, golden-brown fritters perfect for a rainy afternoon snack.

View Ingredients & Method

Ingredients: 2 pkts Maggi, gram flour, rice flour, onion, cabbage, ginger-garlic paste, ajwain, chaat masala.

Method: Boil noodles (al dente). Mix with all ingredients and flour. Fry spoonfuls until golden and crispy.

3. Garlic Butter Cheese-Burst Maggi

A decadent, creamy infusion of garlic butter and melted mozzarella.

View Ingredients & Method

Ingredients: 1 pkt Maggi, 1c water, 0.5c milk, 2tbsp butter, garlic, mozzarella, oregano, chili flakes.

Method: Sauté garlic in butter. Add water, noodles, and tastemaker. Add milk once reduced. Top with cheese and spices, cover until melted.

4. Spicy Schezwan Maggi Stir-Fry

High-heat fusion stir-fry combining fiery Schezwan sauce with classic masala.

View Ingredients & Method

Ingredients: 1 pkt Maggi, oil, carrots, capsicum, spring onion, Schezwan sauce, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar.

Method: Parboil noodles. Stir-fry aromatics and veggies. Add sauces and noodles, toss on high heat for a smoky char.
