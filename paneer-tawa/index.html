Paneer Tawa

An artisan collection of recipes celebrating the smoky, charred, and aromatic magic of the Indian Tawa.

1. Classic Dhaba-Style Paneer Tawa Masala

Focusing on a semi-dry, smoky gravy prepared on a flat griddle to caramelize the aromatics.

Ingredients

  • 300g Paneer cubes
  • Butter/Ghee, Onion, Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Tomatoes, Capsicum, Cumin
  • Pav Bhaji Masala, Kasuri Methi, Cream

Method

1. Sauté cumin, onion, and aromatics until golden. 2. Mash tomatoes with spices. 3. Toss in paneer and capsicum. 4. Finish with cream and kasuri methi for the perfect char.

2. Smoky Tawa Paneer Tikka Sliders

A fusion street-food take using marinated paneer seared on a griddle and served in buttered buns.

Ingredients

  • 250g Paneer slabs
  • Hung curd, Besan, Tandoori Masala
  • Slider buns, Mustard oil, Chaat Masala

Method

1. Marinate paneer in yogurt and spices. 2. Sear on hot tawa until charred. 3. Toast buttered buns on the same griddle. 4. Assemble with green chutney and onions.

3. Paneer Tawa Pulao

A fragrant, spicy rice dish where paneer and vegetables are tossed with pre-cooked rice on high heat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 150g Paneer cubes, Green peas, Carrot
  • Pav Bhaji Masala, Fresh mint/coriander

Method

1. Sizzle spices and vegetables on high heat. 2. Toss in paneer and spice paste. 3. Gently fold in rice with mint and coriander. 4. Finish with lemon.

4. Amritsari Paneer Khurchan

"Khurchan" means "scraped." A technique involving scraping caramelized juices back into the dish.

Ingredients

  • 300g Paneer strips
  • Mustard oil, Ajwain, Bell peppers
  • Tomato ketchup, Garam masala, Cream

Method

1. Sauté aromatics and veggies. 2. Add paneer and let caramelize. 3. "Khurch" (scrape) the crust multiple times. 4. Drizzle with cream to emulsify.
