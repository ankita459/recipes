Garlic Butter Crust Pizza Planks

Elevate simple pizza bread into a textured delicacy with a double-toast method.

Method

  1. Preheat to 400°F.
  2. Mix garlic butter and coat bread.
  3. Toast 5-7 mins.
  4. Top with sauce, cheeses, and flakes.
  5. Bake 10-12 mins, broil 1 min for char.

Deep-Dish Pull-Apart Pizza Boule

An interactive centerpiece, perfect for shared dining experiences.

Method

  1. Cut 1-inch grid into sourdough boule.
  2. Drizzle sauce and stuff with cheese bits.
  3. Brush with seasoned butter mix.
  4. Foil-bake 15 mins, open-bake 10 mins.

Rustic Pizza-Stuffed Cheesy Braids

Refined presentation using dough ropes locked with classic pizza flavors.

Method

  1. Roll dough into 10x15 rectangle.
  2. Fill, seal, and braid three ropes.
  3. Brush with egg wash and top with seasonings.
  4. Bake at 425°F for 18-22 minutes.

Upside-Down Skillet Cheese Bread Pizza

Inspired by Detroit-style, featuring a caramelized "frico" cheese crust.

Method

  1. Layer cheese in cast iron skillet.
  2. Top with sauce, bread slices, and herbs.
  3. Stovetop heat (3 mins), then oven bake (15 mins).
  4. Flip to expose the crispy cheese.
