01. Classic Kolhapuri Misal

"A traditional, fiery moth bean sprout curry served with soft bread and crunchy toppings."

Instructions:

  1. Pressure cook moth beans with turmeric/salt.
  2. Sauté mustard seeds, curry leaves, and aromatics.
  3. Develop flavor with tomato puree and Misal masala.
  4. Simmer until the "kat" (red oil) forms.
  5. Assemble with farsan, onions, and lemon.

02. Misal Pav Bruschetta

"A modern appetizer where toasted pav serves as a crisp base for a concentrated sprout topping."

Instructions:

  1. Sauté boiled sprouts with garam masala and chili.
  2. Butter and toast pav slices until dark gold.
  3. Top pav with sprout mixture.
  4. Garnish with raw onions, tomato, and farsan.

03. Matki & Farsan Salad

"A light, warm salad that highlights the textures of the ingredients without the heavy gravy."

Instructions:

  1. Steam matki sprouts until tender.
  2. Flash fry with mustard seeds and green chilies.
  3. Combine with fresh tomatoes and cilantro.
  4. Fold in farsan and toasted pav croutons before serving.

04. Cheesy Misal Sliders

"A decadent fusion snack where spicy sprouts and melted cheese are grilled inside the pav."

Instructions:

  1. Prepare a thick, dry sprout filling.
  2. Layer inside slit pav with onions and farsan.
  3. Add generous grated cheese.
  4. Grill on a buttered tawa until golden and melted.
